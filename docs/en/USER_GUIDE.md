# User guide: one day and a longer journey

## First launch

On Windows, onboarding optionally collects a goal, subjects and weekday/weekend minutes. You may skip it. It enables no music, Spotify or activity tracking. Templates and examples are not your grades or history. Check backup policy and the data directory first. For English, open Appearance, choose English, save edits and apply/reload.

## A small daily workflow

1. Create a Journey in Roadmap, such as twelve weeks of foundational study. Organize it into subjects or phases and enter realistic capacity.
2. Create a dated task with an estimate in Today. Start focus and pause for breaks. Locking stops accumulation. Resume manually after returning.
3. Complete and finish to keep actual duration. Do not mark unfinished work complete for the statistics.
4. Capture questions and thinking in quick notes or Quick Capture. Full journals support Markdown, tags and relationships.
5. Compare plans and actual progress in Insights and write your own reflection. Rest/illness markers preserve existing progress.

## Broad use cases

| Context | Starting structure | Facts worth keeping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Postgraduate exams | Mathematics, language and specialist subjects with study phases | Real practice, mistake causes and estimate differences |
| Civil-service exams | Reasoning topics, essay practice and fixed mock exams | Topic time, review notes and completion |
| Language learning | Reading, listening, speaking tasks and journals | Materials, questions and manual time |
| Research | Paper/experiment journeys, deadlines and notebooks | Derivations, sources, failed attempts and output links |
| Career and portfolio | Skill trees, interview problems and milestones | Coding attempts, certificates, works and personal contributions |

Edit goals and templates freely. Planning uses your entered capacity and constraints. It does not judge effort or predict examination outcomes.

## Module and appearance controls

Advanced settings provide enable, hide, pin and order. Hide affects navigation only. Disable gates a module page without deleting records. Pinned groups appear first and support drag or keyboard move buttons within the group. Appearance owns theme/accent configuration centrally. Kernel security always remains active and required Today cannot be disabled.

## Records and sharing

Vault works default to private. Explicit public works can enter Profile's allowlisted projection. Save the profile, inspect the projection, then manually export a website. Local preview is not publication. Journals, raw focus, music history, attachments and private relationships stay outside that export. Private JSON/CSV/Markdown may contain sensitive content and belong only in locations you trust.

## Protecting records

Full backups include database, settings and attachments. Portable JSON exchanges data and is not a complete attachment backup. Restore rehearsal opens an isolated directory without replacing the current database. On verification/disk errors, preserve originals. Do not delete DB/WAL or reinitialize storage as a repair. Trash is generally reversible.

## Current limits

Windows is the primary accepted platform. Native Today/Focus are unavailable in experimental macOS/Linux packages. Media observation and several OS-opening helpers are unsupported there. Community execution, a complete marketplace, AI calls, mobile apps and ICS import do not exist. English covers existing frontend copy. OS dialogs follow system language and some Rust tray/notification text remains Chinese.

## Finding Daily fortune

Use the sidebar or “Read today’s sign” in Today’s secondary column. Turn it off in Settings → Advanced → Module management. Fixed signs rotate by local date for entertainment, with no birthday, network or planner effect.
